# STORYBOARD — shot by shot (from the reference video)

Reference: 720×1280 vertical recording, ~10s, laptop on a wooden desk, screen showing the site. Below, `p` = scroll progress of the pinned hero (0 → 1).

## global chrome (always on)
- **navbar** (transparent, blur on scroll): logo mark + "solid fitness" stacked left · links: home, about, services ▾ (muscle gain, weight loss, cross fit, strength, cardio), gallery, plans, contact · right: login, search icon, filled pill `book a free trial`.
- **dots**: bottom-centre, 7 dots, active one wider. Clickable.
- **fab**: bottom-right 48px circle, whatsapp. Gentle pulse every 6s.
- **grain + vignette** overlay, 6% opacity.

## 0 · loader
Black. Logo mark draws in (stroke → fill). Counter 000 → 100 in display font. Curtain splits up, revealing the gym door view.

## 1 · arrival (p 0.00–0.14)
Camera at gym entrance, wide, eye level, floor reflections. Giant two-line headline on the far wall in perspective: **"your gym / deserves more."** Small tag mid-right: `solid fitness`. Slow dolly forward.

## 2 · identity (0.14–0.30)
Camera moves down the main aisle. **"solid fitness"** floats mid-air, huge, slightly translucent white with soft glow; camera passes through the letters (they blur and part). Sub-label `performance`.

## 3 · purpose (0.30–0.46)
Camera pitches up beneath the ceiling grid, glowing LED strips converge. **"train with purpose."** appears mounted on the ceiling, letters readable in perspective. Lights streak past.

## 4 · performance zone (0.46–0.60)
Power rack + oak platform ahead. A **frosted glass card** floats left-of-centre: title `performance zone`, 3 lines of body (TODO copy), buttons `explore` (filled accent pill) and `see equipment` (outlined ghost pill). Background parallaxes opposite to the card.

## 5 · memberships (0.60–0.78)
Camera glides sideways; a row of **three tilted glass cards** rises into frame at staggered depths:
- `core` — portrait of a trainer, 2 lines, pill `start your free trial`
- `pro` — centre, slightly larger, in front
- `elite` — rightmost, brightest rim light
Cursor tilts all three (±8°), hover lifts the card +24px and lights its border with `--accent`. Click goes to membership section anchored to that tier.

## 6 · dollhouse (0.78–1.00)
Camera climbs and rotates to top-down. Walls and ceiling fade, revealing the full gym floor plan in a dark cutaway with warm edge lights. Headline: **"train beyond limits."** centred over the plan. CTA `start free trial`. Left/right arrows to step through zones. Bottom: thumbnail strip of zones (cardio, strength, cross fit, weight loss, muscle gain) that highlights the matching area on the plan on hover.

## after the hero
Hero un-pins, footer-height spacer fades the canvas to `--bg`, normal sections begin (trainers, transformations, fuel, membership, find us, footer).

## transitions
Each beat overlaps the previous by 6% for a cross-dissolve. Add a 2–3px focus pull (blur in/out) on the sequence canvas at every boundary.

## mobile
Same 7 beats, portrait crop of frames (object-position per beat), cards stack vertically with snap, dollhouse becomes a vertical pan.
